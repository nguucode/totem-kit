# Spinner

Says that something is loading when there is no telling how long it will
take, or, given a value, how far along it is. Spec:
[uiguideline.com/components/spinner](https://www.uiguideline.com/components/spinner).
A plain element: there is no behaviour here worth Base UI.

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.Variants} />

## Anatomy

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Track** | The full circle, `--border`. Frames the motion; decoration. |
| **Indicator** | The arc: a quarter turn spinning, or the value as a share of the ring. Coloured by `variant`. |
| **Label** | `label`, under the indicator. Also the accessible name. |

## Props

| Prop | Values | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `size` | `sm`, `md`, `lg` | `md` | 16, 24, 32px. |
| `variant` | `primary`, `secondary`, `neutral` | `neutral` | Colour of the indicator. |
| `label` | string | – | Visible text and accessible name; `"Loading"` when omitted. |
| `delay` | number (ms) | `0` | Renders nothing until it has passed. |
| `value` | 0–100 | – | Turns it into a progress ring. Clamped. |
| `isIndeterminate` | boolean | `false` | Spin even when `value` is given. |

Any other prop goes to the outer `<span>`.

<Controls of={SpinnerStories.Default} />

### Design notes

- **`neutral` is the default variant**, in `--foreground`: a spinner is
  most often a quiet placeholder inside content that has its own colour.
- **`value`**: A load that can report progress should, and a
  ring reads better than a bar in a small space such as a button or a
  thumbnail.
- **`delay`**: Most loads finish in under half a second; a
  spinner that appears and vanishes in 200ms is a flash, not information.

## Accessibility

- **Spinning, it is `role="status"`**, a polite live region, named by
  `label` or "Loading". Screen readers announce it when it appears
  without interrupting.
- **With a value, it is `role="progressbar"`** with `aria-valuenow`,
  `aria-valuemin="0"` and `aria-valuemax="100"`.
- The SVG is hidden; the name comes from `aria-label`, so a visible
  `label` is not read twice.
- The indicator clears 3:1 on the page in every variant: `--primary-text`
  (measured at 4.5:1 per accent), `--muted-foreground`, `--foreground`.
  The track is decoration and may be faint.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it slows to one turn in 2.5s rather than
  stopping: a frozen arc reads as "stuck".
- Put `aria-busy="true"` on the region that is loading, and remove the
  spinner when the load ends, so the change is announced.
